# TOP-RSCH-005 bounded evidence question

**Question:** Is `Topology` usable as the working/public name for an open modeler editor, and which package IDs, repository names, domains, and store titles avoid obvious collisions?

**Research mode:** documentation/source survey only. No production behavior, public registration, publication, purchase, account reservation, or rights-holder contact was performed.

**Approved source order:** trademark databases; Apple App Store and Google Play; GitHub/GitLab; crates.io and pub.dev; music/audio/modeler products; domains and social handles.

**Required evidence:** direct source URL, access date, exact/adjacent use, jurisdiction or platform relevance, engineering risk label, keep/modify/replace recommendation, provisional unpublished IDs, explicit uncertainties/counsel boundary, and follow-up ADR/packet names.

**Bounded limitation:** WIPO's interactive search could not be queried because Altcha blocked deterministic interaction. This is recorded as a limitation; it does not turn the report into legal clearance or a comprehensive search.
