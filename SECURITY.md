# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not publish exploit details, raw secrets, or attack payloads in issues,
fixtures, commits, or discussions. When a public repository and a private
security-reporting channel exist, use that channel. Until then, contact the
project maintainer through the canonical private contact published with the
repository and provide a minimal reproducible report.

## Scope and handling

Security reports may cover repository tooling, local storage, transport
boundaries, credentials, release artifacts, or dependencies. Acknowledgement,
triage, disclosure timing, and fixes are not promised by this bootstrap file;
they require an active maintainer and a published contact channel. Never test a
device, service, or account you do not own or have permission to assess.
