# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, specific, and
honest. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal campaigns, doxxing, and
review-bombing or incitement against people or projects are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement belongs in evidence, reproducible reports, and proposed
fixes—not personal attacks. Do not use this project to target vendors,
competitors, upstream maintainers, or community members.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this policy. In
this private bootstrap phase, report conduct concerns through the private
maintainer channel announced with any future public repository.
